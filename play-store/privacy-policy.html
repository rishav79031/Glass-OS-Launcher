Glass OS Launcher Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Glass OS Launcher is a home screen app for Android, made by androspace. This policy explains what the app accesses and what happens to it. In short: everything stays on your device, except an approximate location sent to fetch the weather.

Information the app does not collect

Glass OS Launcher has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not send your apps, notifications, calendar, photos or settings anywhere. There is no server run by the developer.

Permissions and how they are used

  • Approximate location (optional): used only to show the weather for where you are. A location rounded to about 1 km is sent to the MET Norway weather service (api.met.no) to get the forecast. It is not stored by the app beyond the last forecast, and you can instead type a city name.
  • Calendar (optional, read-only): shows your upcoming events in the calendar widgets. Events are read on your device and never sent anywhere.
  • Notification access (optional): shows badge counts on app icons, your notifications in Notification Centre, and the playing song in Control Centre. Notification content stays on your device and is never stored or sent.
  • Display over other apps (optional): lets you open Control Centre with a swipe while using other apps.
  • Modify system settings and Do Not Disturb access (optional): let the Control Centre brightness slider, rotation lock and Focus button work.
  • Screen recording: when you start a recording from Control Centre, Android asks for your permission each time. The video is saved only to your device's Movies folder.
  • Photos: photos you pick for wallpapers or the Photos widget are copied into the app's private storage on your device. The app has no access to your other photos.
  • Physical Activity (optional): used locally by the Activity widget to track daily steps via your device's built-in step counter sensor. Activity data remains exclusively on your device and is never transmitted or shared.
  • App list: as a home screen, the app reads the list of installed apps on your device to show them. This list is not sent anywhere.

Third-party services

Weather data comes from the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (MET Norway), licensed under CC BY 4.0. Their privacy statement is at met.no.

Children

Glass Launcher is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Your choices

Every permission is optional and can be turned off in Android Settings at any time. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your device.
