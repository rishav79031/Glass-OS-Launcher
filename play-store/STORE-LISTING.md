**What's new in v1.65**

**What's new in v1.64**

• Native Keyguard Unlock: Removed duplicate lockscreen activity on screen-off; Android native keyguard handles wake and sleep smoothly with zero double fingerprint prompts.
• Refined Notification Centre: Removed fingerprint button from the bottom bar and eliminated dark ambient gradient overlays for pure authentic wallpaper transparency.
• Mobile Control Centre Optimization: Swiping down from top-edge on mobile routes directly to Notification Centre. Control Centre indicator and strip disabled on phones.
• Tablet Status Bar Notification Icons: Status bar renders app icons for active notifications beside time and date on tablets.

**What's new in v1.63**
• Hardware-Aware Biometric Unlock: Fingerprint sensor button is only presented on devices with supported biometric hardware and enrolled fingerprints, launching the native BiometricPrompt sensor on tap.
• Ultra-Transparent Liquid Glass Notifications: Highly transparent frosted glass notification cards that let your lockscreen wallpaper shine through with crisp luminous refraction borders.
• Height-Stepped Notification Stacks: Stacked notifications now clearly layer with distinct vertical height tiers (one card above, subsequent cards stepping below in height), smoothly expanding into rows upon tapping.
• Precision Screen Time Tracking: Accurate Digital Wellbeing foreground timing queried directly from UsageEvents API with midnight 00:00 AM – Present counter.
• Pedometer 10-Step Consecutive Filter: Eliminates micro-steps and false positive fidgeting; only registers verified continuous walking bouts of 10+ steps.
• Active Call Dynamic Island Dropdown: Tapping the active call Dynamic Island expands a sleek in-call HUD with Mute, Speaker, Disconnect, and Open App controls.

**News app:** No. **Government app:** No. **Financial features:** None. **Health:** No.

**Data safety:**
- Does your app collect or share user data? **Yes** (only approximate location, for weather).
- Data type: **Location > Approximate location**
  - Collected: Yes. Shared: No (sent to the weather service only to provide the feature).
  - Processed ephemerally: Yes.
  - Required or optional: **Optional** (users can type a city instead).
  - Purpose: **App functionality**.

- Request delete packages: the Remove App option in the icon menu, as in other launchers.
- Modify system settings: brightness slider and rotation lock in Control Centre.

## Release steps
1. Create a Google Play Console developer account (one-time fee) and verify your identity.
2. Create app: name "Glass Launcher", App, Free, English (United Kingdom or United States).
3. Fill in App content (answers above) and Store listing (texts and images above).
4. New personal accounts must first run a **closed test with at least 12 testers for 14 days**. Upload the .aab to Closed testing, add testers' Gmail addresses, and share the opt-in link.
5. After 14 days, apply for production access in the Dashboard, then promote the release to Production.
6. Accept **Play App Signing** when uploading the first bundle. Keep `keystore/glass-release.jks` and `keystore.properties` backed up: it's your upload key for every future update.
