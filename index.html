Privacy Policy for Glass OS Launcher

Effective Date: November 2, 2026

Thank you for choosing to use Glass OS Launcher ("App"). This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We respect your privacy. Glass OS Launcher is designed to run locally on your device. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information, location data, usage statistics, or user analytics.

2. Permissions Used by the App

To deliver its core features as a home screen launcher, the App may request standard Android permissions:

  • Home Screen / Launcher Role: To replace your device's default home screen interface and display your installed applications and widgets.
  • Query All Packages / Installed Apps: Required locally on your device solely to display your list of installed apps inside the app drawer. This data never leaves your device.

3. Third-Party Services

Because the App does not collect personal data or integrate external telemetry/tracking SDKs, no user data is passed to third-party advertisers or analytics providers.

4. Children's Privacy

Our App does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy page.
